3 · Product Tier Structure

Every product follows one of three tiers. Keep it simple — customers pick a tier, not a catalog.

GOOD — Basic

Standard options, popular colors, common sizes. Online pricing and purchase. Fast turnaround.

BETTER — Premier

Wider selection, specialty materials, more colors. Online pricing or contact us. Some need consultation.

BEST — Hunter Douglas / Luxury

Hunter Douglas products only. Always consultation. Never instant price. White glove in-home service.

Hunter Douglas — separate section on every page

Hunter Douglas gets its own prominent button/card on the shades page.
Clicking it shows: Silhouette, Pirouette, Luminette, Vignette, Duette, Sonnette, Skyline.
Every HD product → "Request consultation" only. No pricing. Ever.
Copy: "Hunter Douglas is our luxury line. Every product is custom quoted with an in-home consultation."

6 · PDF Reading Instructions

Read all PDFs in the pdfs/ folder BEFORE simplifying any product pages. Use the content to write accurate descriptions — not generic copy.

From each PDF extract:

  • Collection and product names
  • Key differences between tiers or collections
  • Available colors, textures, materials
  • Size limitations and maximums
  • Special features and options
⚠ NEVER copy pricing from PDFs onto any page. PDFs in pdfs/wholesale-pricing/ are internal reference only — never link publicly.

Use extracted info to populate the Basic vs Premier descriptions accurately. For example — if woven woods PDF shows "Galaxy Collection" and "Bali Collection" with specific color families, use those actual names.

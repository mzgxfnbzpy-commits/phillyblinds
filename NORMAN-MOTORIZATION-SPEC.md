## THREE motor systems exist (p.2 TOC)
1. **Norman Smart Motorization** (pp.4–59) — the premium/default system.
2. **Automate Home Motorization** (pp.60–73) — Norman's rebadged **Rollease Acmeda Automate** system (uses "Automate ARC Motor", Automate 12V DC, Automate hub). NOT compatible with Norman Smart — controls must be ordered per-program (p.3).
3. **AutoWand Motorization** (pp.74–91) — budget, wand-operated, no remote (p.74).

> ⚠️ Business-vs-catalog note: the PDF lists all three. Our site currently offers **Norman Smart + "Rollease Acmeda Automate"** and does NOT offer AutoWand. Also, Norman's own name is **"Automate Home"**, but our site labels it **"Rollease Acmeda Automate."** Decide (a) whether to add AutoWand, and (b) one consistent name for system #2.

## ✅ CHARGING WAND — the only "wand" we offer (owner-confirmed 7/12)

> The owner's entire "AutoWand / corded-vs-cordless / extension" discussion was about the **Charging Wand** — the wand that recharges the shade's battery. There is **NO separate AutoWand motor** on our site (consistent with CLAUDE.md "no AutoWand"). The standalone AutoWand *motor* system (pp.74–91) exists in the PDF but we do NOT offer it.

The Charging Wand is a way to recharge the **Norman Smart rechargeable battery**. It is **for battery-powered motors only — not AC-adapter systems**. Max channels per shade/motor: 5.

- **Availability: Honeycomb (Cellular) + Roller ONLY** (never with a Cassette headrail or Dual/coupled shade). **Recommend it primarily for Cellular.**
- **Wireless Charging Wand (cordless) — $428**: built-in Li-ion battery; charges the shade wirelessly; includes 1 wireless wand + 1 charger for the wand; 35.5"; black. **No extension cable** (self-contained).
- **Wired Charging Wand — $161**: no battery, must stay plugged in; includes 1 wired wand + 1 charging connector (~59") **+ 1 extension cable (~78.74")** → total reach ~137.74"; 35.5"; black. **This is the one that takes the extension.**
- **36" Extension Pole — $75**: snap-on, extends **either** wand to 72" total; black.
- On **every other product**, the rechargeable battery is recharged by the **included AC-adapter charger** — nothing for the customer to choose or pay for separately.

   - Optional **DC connection harness $11**, extra **chargers**.

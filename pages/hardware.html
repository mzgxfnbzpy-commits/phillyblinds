Step 1
What type of hardware?
Decorative hardware is meant to be seen — beautiful rods, rings, and finials. Functional/non-decorative hardware is engineered for smooth operation and is typically hidden behind a valance or header.
✨
Decorative
Rods, rings, and finials that are part of the design. Visible, beautiful, and available in dozens of finishes and styles.
Select · Orion · Kirsch · Paris Texas · The Finial Company
⚙️
Non-decorative / Traverse
Engineered traverse tracks and systems built for smooth, reliable operation. Ideal for motorized drapery, large windows, or where the hardware will be hidden.
Kirsch Architrac · Custom traverse systems

Our story
Three generations, one trade

Philly Blinds started with a simple idea: bring the same level of craft that Michael Healy spent 60 years perfecting to homeowners who want more than a big-box experience. Michael began his career in window treatments before many of today's products even existed — learning every stitch, measurement, and mechanism from the ground up.

His son Justin joined the trade and launched Michael J. Healy Installations LLC in 2014, building the business around the same values: precision measuring, honest pricing, and installations that are done right the first time. Sarah Healy rounds out the team with a sharp eye for design and fabrication, helping clients find the fabric, style, and finish that fits their home.

We design, fabricate, and install every product we sell. That means one point of contact from the first measurement to the last bracket — no subcontractors, no handoffs, no surprises.

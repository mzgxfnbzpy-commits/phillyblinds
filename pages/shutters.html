4 Mount & frame situation

Inside mount sits within the window opening. Outside mount covers the full opening or wall area. Direct mount hinges directly to the window frame — not for drywall.

Inside mount minimum depth Measure the depth of your window opening. 1⅞″ louvers need ~¾″ clearance; 4½″ louvers need ~1¾″. We'll confirm during measurement.

7 T-Posts

T-posts are stationary center dividers. Vertical T-posts split the shutter width. Horizontal T-posts split double-hung panels. Required when your layout includes "T".

Horizontal T-post default type: Chamfer T-post unless otherwise specified. Custom T-post width up to 7½″ available (surcharge applies). Section length per T-post ≤48″.

8 Louver size

Larger louvers mean a clearer view when open and a bolder look. Smaller louvers offer a traditional style.

9 Tilt rod / louver control
⚠ InvisibleTilt™ is not available with 1⅞″ louvers. Please select a larger louver size or choose a different tilt type.
InvisibleTilt™ embeds the tilt mechanism inside the stile — no visible rod. Surcharge applies. Not available for 1⅞″ louvers.
10 Frame style

The frame surrounds the shutter panel. Most inside-mount orders use an L Frame. Z frames work for window casings with specific depth. Hang strips are low-profile.

11 Divider rails

A divider rail adds a horizontal mid-panel rail so louvers can be controlled independently above and below. Required on tall panels.

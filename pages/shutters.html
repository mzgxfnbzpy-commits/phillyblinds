Material / Construction
Real Wood — Normandy®
Solid hardwood (painted) or Phoenix wood (stained). 24 paint colors, 18 stain colors, 6 OSMO oil-wax finishes. Mortise & tenon joinery. Lifetime limited warranty. Max panel height 132″.
Composite — Woodlore®
Wood composite core with polypropylene coating — resists cupping, warping, and moisture. 6 solid paint colors. Reinforced engineered stiles. Lifetime limited warranty. Max panel height 120″.
Hybrid — Woodlore® Plus
Composite frame with ABS co-polymer louvers for maximum dimensional stability. 24 paint colors (3 with surcharge). Optional waterproof version with ABS frame & stainless hinges. Max panel height 120″.
Not sure which material is right for your home? We'll recommend the best option at your free in-home consultation based on room conditions, budget, and style preferences.
Louver size
Larger louvers = bigger view-through and more contemporary look. 3½″ is the most popular. 1⅞″ elliptical is the slimmest option. Max single-panel width varies by louver size — confirmed at your measurement visit.

Panel operation
Stack / open direction
Single panel: swings one direction. Pair: two panels share the opening — split center is most common for standard windows. Bi-fold and sliding track are ideal for wide openings and sliding/French doors.
Frame style & coverage
Frame profile
L-frame is standard for most windows. Z-frame goes over existing trim. Hidden frame is a premium flush-mount option. No frame / Deco mount attaches panels directly to the wall or reveals.
Frame sides (which sides of the window get a frame)
Full perimeter = frame on all 4 sides. L/T/R = left, top, and right (common when sill stays exposed). L/R = left and right sides only. Your installer will confirm the best option at the measurement visit.
Tilt rod
Norman Motorization only. Norman plantation shutters use Norman’s own integrated motorized tilt system — third-party motors (Lutron, Somfy, etc.) are not compatible with Norman shutter tilt mechanisms.
Motorized tilt pricing included in your custom quote. Requires power source at the window.

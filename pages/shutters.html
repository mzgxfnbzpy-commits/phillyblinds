Material / Construction
Real Wood — Normandy®
Premium Phoenixwood / Paulownia hardwood. Natural grain, warmth, and a true luxury feel. Available stained or painted. Best for low-humidity living spaces.
Composite — Woodlore®
Engineered MDF core with a durable polypropylene coating. Moisture-resistant and warp-resistant — ideal for bathrooms, kitchens, and high-humidity rooms. Painted finishes only.
Hybrid — Woodlore® Plus
The best of both worlds. Composite frame with lightweight ABS louvers for superior moisture resistance and durability. The ultimate solution for bathrooms, kitchens, and humid climates.
Not sure which material is right for your home? We'll recommend the best option at your free in-home consultation based on room conditions, budget, and style preferences.
Louver size
Larger louvers provide more view-through and a more contemporary look. 3.5" is the most popular.
Panel configuration
Full height
Covers the entire window in one panel set.
Café style
Covers lower half only. Keeps top open for light and view.
Tier-on-tier
Top and bottom panels operate independently.
Special shape
Arched tops, circles, angles, French doors. Always custom.
Special shape shutters are always custom-engineered. Please describe your window shape in the notes field at the bottom of the form.
Frame style
L-frame is standard for most windows. Z-frame goes over existing trim. Hidden frame is a premium flush-mount option.
Tilt rod
Norman Motorization only. Norman plantation shutters use Norman’s own integrated motorized tilt system — third-party motors (Lutron, Somfy, etc.) are not compatible with Norman shutter tilt mechanisms.
Motorized tilt pricing included in your custom quote. Requires power source at the window.

Shutter measuring uses 3W × 3H rules

Unlike shades, shutters require 3 width measurements and 3 height measurements per window. Deductions vary depending on your chosen frame style. Our team handles all measuring during the free in-home visit — or describe your windows and we'll guide you.
